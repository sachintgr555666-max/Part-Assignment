## 🧹 Data Cleaning & Preparation

The following steps were performed in **Excel**:

- Removed duplicate records and handled missing/null values
- Standardized date formats and corrected data types
- Filtered out irrelevant or erroneous entries
- Created calculated columns for metrics such as total revenue, profit margin, and sales growth
- Structured the data into clean tabular format ready for analysis

---

## 🔍 Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

Key questions explored during the analysis:

- What is the overall sales trend over time?
- Which products or categories generate the most revenue?
- Which regions are top performers vs. underperformers?
- Are there seasonal patterns in sales?
- What is the relationship between discounts and sales volume?

---

## 💡 Key Insights

- **Insight 1:** Sales show a consistent upward trend, with the highest revenue recorded in Q4 driven by seasonal demand.
- **Insight 2:** A small number of top-selling products account for the majority of total revenue (Pareto effect).
- **Insight 3:** Certain regions significantly underperform compared to the national average, indicating gaps in market penetration.
- **Insight 4:** Higher discount rates correlate with increased volume but reduced profit margins in some categories.

---

## 🚀 Recommendations

- **Focus on Q4 preparation** — allocate more inventory and marketing budget ahead of the peak sales season.
- **Double down on top products** — prioritize stock and promotions for high-performing SKUs.
- **Investigate underperforming regions** — conduct targeted campaigns or review pricing strategies in low-performing areas.
- **Optimize discount strategy** — set a discount ceiling to protect margins while still driving volume.

## ⚙️ How to Use

### Excel File
1. Open the `.xlsx` file in Microsoft Excel (2016 or later recommended).
2. Navigate through the sheets: `Raw Data` → `Cleaned Data` → `Analysis`.
3. All formulas and calculated columns are included and ready to view.

### Power BI File
1. Open the `.pbix` file in **Power BI Desktop** (free download from Microsoft).
2. The dashboard will load with all visuals and filters pre-configured.
3. Use the slicers and filters on the report page to explore the data interactively.

> **Note:** No additional data connections are required — all data is embedded in the files.
